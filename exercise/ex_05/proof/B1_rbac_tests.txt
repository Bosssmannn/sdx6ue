==== Test 6: dev CANNOT modify accounts (expect PermissionDenied) ====
$ argocd account update-password --account admin --new-password "Hack123!" --current-password "wrong"

CONCLUSION: The 'developer' role has the principle of least privilege properly enforced.
It can view and sync applications (the developer's day-to-day needs)
but cannot delete applications, modify ArgoCD settings, or escalate privileges.
